# Indian Company Travel Policy — Illustrative Draft Sample

**Status: Draft example only.** These figures are a practical starting point for a fictional Indian company, not an India-wide statutory or industry standard, and not an approved policy for this organization. An authorized Admin/Finance owner must review the amounts, city tiers, employee levels, taxes, and accounting treatment before publication. Until then, these values must not block or approve a real expense request.

## Suggested domestic travel matrix (INR)

| Employee band | Hotel per night — Metro tier | Hotel per night — Other cities | Meals per travel day | Local conveyance per day |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Staff / Individual contributor | ₹4,000 | ₹2,500 | ₹800 | ₹600 |
| Department lead / Manager | ₹5,500 | ₹3,500 | ₹1,200 | ₹1,000 |
| Senior leadership | ₹8,000 | ₹5,500 | ₹1,800 | ₹1,500 |

The company must define which cities belong to each tier and whether caps include GST. The table deliberately does not prescribe airfare, rail class, mileage reimbursement, foreign travel, or statutory tax treatment; Finance should add those only after reviewing the actual company policy and accounting setup.

## Control rules to configure

1. **Advance ceiling:** an advance may be requested up to 80% of the approved trip estimate in this sample. The approved estimate is the reference amount; this is not an automatic entitlement.
2. **One open advance:** a requester may have only one unsettled travel advance at a time. A new advance stays blocked until settlement, cancellation, or an authorized exception resolves the prior one.
3. **Over-cap handling:** do not silently clamp the requested amount. Create an explicit policy-exception review for Finance/Admin with the requested amount, applicable cap, reason, evidence, and decision trail. Approval must not bypass the normal expense approval chain.
4. **Department flexibility:** a department lead may propose department-specific caps at or below the company limits. The proposal remains pending until Admin/Finance reviews and publishes it. A department override cannot raise the company ceiling.
5. **Locations:** requests select an active location from the organization’s approved client/site accounting catalogue. A missing location is submitted as a location-add request for Admin/Accounting review; it is not accepted as free text on the expense claim.
6. **Audit trail:** retain policy version, effective date, requester, department, project (optional), selected location ID, estimate, exception reason/decision, advance, and settlement references with the request.

## Publication checklist

- Confirm employee bands and mapping from authenticated role/profile data.
- Confirm Metro/Other city classification and hotel, meal, and local travel limits.
- Confirm GST/receipt rules and the finance category/account codes with Finance.
- Confirm approvers and escalation route for policy exceptions.
- Confirm the single-open-advance rule and settlement/cancellation states.
- Load approved client/site locations from the accounting master; assign an owner for location requests.
- Publish a version with an effective date; test that Web and Android read the same policy and that server-side validation rejects stale or over-limit requests correctly.
